**When Life Gives You Tangerines** is a 2025 K-drama on Netflix, and it's my favorite series at the moment. It follows two people from Jeju Island through several decades of their lives, and it honestly feels like watching a whole lifetime in 16 episodes.[^1]

> "Life is hard, but it's worth living."

### Reasons Why I Love It

1. The story spans a whole lifetime, so you really grow with the characters
2. The acting is so natural that it never feels like they're acting
3. The context of the drama is heartwarming at the same time a realization to reality

### Themes To Look Out For While Watching

- Love and family
- Hardship and resilience through life
- Growing up and growing old together with loved ones

### Fun Facts

- It's set on Jeju Island, which is famous for its tangerines (hence the title!)
- It's divided into **4 acts** instead of regular episodes
- It made me *cry a lot*, so keep tissues ready!

## Rating

I'd give it 10 out of 10. I would **absolutely** recommend it to anyone, even if you don't usually watch K-dramas hehe.
